Credit & Refund Policy

How Lucky 7 plans to handle paid analysis credits.

Effective date: 3 October 2026.

When a credit is used

A paid Lucky 7 credit is intended to be consumed only after a successful Full Analysis completes.

When a credit should remain available

  • The fixture input is rejected before a paid analysis completes.
  • A technical/server error prevents completion.
  • The system fails while processing the paid request.

NO RESULT

A properly completed analysis can conclude that the evidence does not meet Lucky 7's quality threshold. The final production payment rule for completed NO RESULT outputs will be shown clearly before public payments are enabled.

Payment-processor refunds

Where a refund is required, it is also subject to the rules and technical process of the payment provider used for the purchase and applicable consumer law.

Contact

Users should provide their account email and payment reference when requesting support for a disputed credit.
