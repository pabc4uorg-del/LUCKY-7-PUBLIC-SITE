How Lucky 7 works

Paste, upload or capture fixture information. Lucky 7 checks the fixture, gathers available football data and produces a quality-controlled analysis.

1. Add fixtures

Paste fixture text, upload a text/CSV file, or use screenshot text recognition in the connected Android experience.

2. Check the data

Recent results, goals for/against, home/away context and source quality are reconciled where public data is available.

3. Review analysis

The system returns football analytics with data-quality information and can return NO RESULT when the evidence is not good enough.

Analytics-only policy

Lucky 7 is a football analytics service. It does not provide betting accounts, bookmaker links, staking instructions, betting slips or guaranteed outcomes.

Our standard is the same for free and paid analysis. Paid use unlocks more product output — it does not receive “better” data or a different reliability standard.

Fixture upload limit: up to 300 raw fixtures per analysis run.

Frequently asked questions

Does Lucky 7 guarantee an outcome?

No. Football is uncertain. Lucky 7 presents statistical analysis and data-quality information, not guarantees.

Is the free analysis lower quality?

No. Free and paid use the same analytical standards and safeguards. The difference is the amount of product output.

When is a paid credit used?

The product is designed to deduct a credit only after a successful paid analysis. Technical failures or rejected runs should not consume a credit.
