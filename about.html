About Lucky 7

A PABC football analytics project.

Lucky 7 Soccer Prediction is created and presented by PABC — The Pan African Boys Club.

The product is designed to turn football fixture information into structured statistical analysis. Its core principles are useful analysis, transparent uncertainty, data-quality checks, graceful failure when data is missing, and no fabricated records.

What we analyse

  • Recent team performance and last-match history where available.
  • Goals scored and conceded.
  • Home and away context.
  • Competition and schedule context.
  • Public-source reliability and data quality.

What Lucky 7 is not

Lucky 7 is not a bookmaker, betting account, wagering platform or gambling service. We do not provide staking instructions, bookmaker links, betting slips or guaranteed outcomes.

Our standard

Free users and paid users should receive the same analytical standards, model safeguards and data-quality rules. Paid use unlocks additional product output rather than a different standard of evidence.
