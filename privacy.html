Privacy Policy

How Lucky 7 plans to handle account and usage information.

Effective date: 3 October 2026.

Information we may collect

When account features are active, Lucky 7 may collect information such as account email, securely stored password hashes, session information, credit balance and ledger entries, fixture text submitted for analysis, product usage records, device/browser information required for security, and technical logs.

Payments

Lucky 7 should not store full card or Mobile Money credentials. Website payments are intended to be processed by Paystack and Android in-app digital purchases by Google Play where required. Lucky 7 may store payment references, status and the number of credits purchased so that the account can be updated.

Fixture screenshots

Where screenshot intake is available, extracted text is used to populate the fixture input for review. Production handling should minimise retention of the original screenshot unless the user explicitly chooses to keep it.

Why data is used

  • Provide and secure user accounts.
  • Process requested football analysis.
  • Maintain credits and purchase records.
  • Detect technical failures and abuse.
  • Improve reliability using aggregated product metrics.

Sharing

We do not plan to sell personal information. Information may be processed by infrastructure, analytics and payment providers when necessary to operate the service.

Children and paid services

Where local law requires an adult to enter a paid digital-services agreement, a parent or guardian should handle the purchase.

Contact

Use the support contact published on the Lucky 7 Contact page once the public contact address is activated.
